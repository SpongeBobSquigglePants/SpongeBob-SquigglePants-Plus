    Tucked away in the game's save files is a stowaway: the save banner and icon of Batman: The Brave and the Bold - The Videogame, WayForward's 2010 Wii game. They sit right next to SpongeBob SquigglePants' own, but the game never uses them: it only asks for its own banner and icons, which the Wii shows in its Data Management screen.

    From what we can tell, SpongeBob SquigglePants' save system was built on Batman's, and these were left behind when it moved in.
